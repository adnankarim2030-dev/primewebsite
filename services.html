Questions

Frequently asked

Can't find your answer? Talk to our team.

Is the first consultation really free?
Yes. The first meeting with our advisory team is free and carries no obligation. We use it to understand what you need and tell you honestly how we can help.
How do you verify a property's title?
Before a property is listed or shown, we check the title documents and allotment papers with the relevant authority and confirm the seller's ownership. We share what we find with you before you commit.
Which areas of Karachi do you cover?
Our core markets are DHA, Clifton, Bahria Town and PECHS, and we also handle property in North Nazimabad, Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Bahadurabad, Shahrah-e-Faisal and other parts of the city.
Can you help me get home financing?
We can estimate instalments with you, explain typical bank requirements and prepare the verified title paperwork banks ask for. Final approval and rates are decided by the bank.
Can you manage my property while I am abroad?
Yes. Our property management service covers buying, selling and renting on your behalf, with regular updates by WhatsApp and email.
