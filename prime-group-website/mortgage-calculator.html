Before you apply

Three things worth knowing

KIBOR benchmark

Pakistani commercial banks price home loans on the Karachi Interbank Offered Rate (KIBOR) plus a margin, so instalments on floating-rate loans can change over time.

Down payment terms

State Bank of Pakistan guidelines generally require a minimum 15–20% down payment for residential property financing.

Verified title guarantee

Prime Group checks property titles and allotment letters upfront, which banks need before they approve a loan.
