Tell us about your plot or building

Share a few details and our construction team will arrange a free consultation, usually starting with a site visit.

Do you build on my own plot?
Yes. Most of our turnkey projects are built on the client's own plot. We can also help you find and verify a plot first.
Can I hire you only for design or only for construction?
Yes. You can engage us for consultation, architecture, construction or project management on its own, or for the complete turnkey package.
How is the cost decided?
Cost depends on covered area, specification and finishing level. After the consultation we give you a written estimate broken down by stage before any work starts.
Will I get updates while I am away?
Yes. Your project manager shares milestone reports with photos by WhatsApp and email, and you can visit the site at any time.
