Before you visit

Common questions

Do I need an appointment to visit the office?
Walk-ins are welcome during office hours, but a quick call or WhatsApp beforehand makes sure the right advisor is available for you.
Can you arrange property viewings?
Yes. Tell us which listing you're interested in and we'll schedule a viewing at a time that suits you, with a video walkthrough if you can't visit in person.
I live abroad. Can you still help?
Yes. We regularly work with overseas Pakistanis by WhatsApp and email, from shortlisting and verification to managing a build or a rental.
How quickly will I hear back?
WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach us. Messages and emails sent outside office hours are answered on the next working day.
